# toy-workshop

Measured from `refs/shot_4.png`, `refs/shot_13.png` and `refs/shot_22.png` (frames the user likes).

## What was taken (the feel)
- **Palette.** Teal plaster walls with a darker lower band, worn blue-teal square tiles with dark grout,
  brass and brushed steel, red-orange painted machine bases, a yellow/black hazard stripe.
  For the twist beat: violet/magenta glow on deep navy.
- **Light.** Cool, soft teal fill from above. One warm caged lamp in the shot. A green-yellow wash grazing the wall.
  Warm highlights and teal shadows.
- **Lens.** About 24-32 mm, mostly high-angle looking down into the room, deep focus on wides.
  A low angle with some blur for the twist shot.
- **Composition.** A small person-scale figure in a set where screws, nuts and pipes are 2-4x their size.
  The figure sits a little left of centre in the middle third.
- **Cutaway.** The machine housing turns to glass with glowing blue edges and spark arcs. Same idea as the
  X-ray rule this channel already uses.

## What was not taken
- No characters. The references have a boy in a cowboy hat with a hi-vis vest, and a hamster in a wheel. Instead
  there's **Tack**, an original clockwork tin worker (see `style.json` → `character`).
- No logos or channel name. One reference shows another channel's watermark, which was ignored. No wording and no sound.
- The references show no captions, so this style uses the channel's own `zack` caption preset.
